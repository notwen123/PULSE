Build with CMC: API Hackathon
CoinMarketCap tracks prices, market caps, exchange volumes, DEX pairs, derivatives and on-chain data across every major chain. All of it is available through the CMC API, and most developers have never made a single call against it.

This hackathon is three weeks to change that. Build something that runs on CMC data. A bot, a dashboard, an agent tool, a screener, a research assistant. Anything a real person would use.

Every participant gets a FREE Startup tier plan for the duration of the event. Higher call credits than the free tier.

The top three keep going: a free year of CMC API access starting the day the hackathon closes, at Professional, Growth, and Startup. Long enough to turn a hackathon project into something that actually runs.

No card, no approval queue.

What You can Build
Four tracks, covering the ways developers actually use market data. Pick one when you submit.

Markets and Trading Tools: screeners, alert bots, scanners, portfolio and PnL trackers
AI Agents and Automation: agent tools, MCP integrations, workflow automations, anything that puts live market data in front of an LLM
Data and Visualisation: dashboards, research tools, comparative analysis, anything that makes the data legible
Real World Assets: tokenised equities, commodities, treasuries and funds, issuer explorers, anything built on the new RWA endpoints
The tracks are chain-neutral on purpose. CoinMarketCap lists every chain, and we are not asking you to pick one.

What Counts as a Submission
Something that runs. A public repo, a working demo or a short screen recording, the endpoints you used named explicitly, and visible evidence of a real API call.

A description of what the API can do is not a submission. Show it working.

Required in Every Submission:

A public repository
A working demo, a deployed link, or a screen recording of it running
Make a X/Twitter post, with a link to your Dorahacks submission, demo video, and the hashtag #BuildwithCMC
The CMC API endpoints you used, named explicitly
Visible evidence of a real API call: code and response
A short note on what the API made possible and where it got in the way
One track selected
Prize Pool
$10K USD worth of Prizes

Free Startup tier plan for every participant.
Full year of CMC API access: Professional, Growth, and Startup (Top 3)
Exclusive CMC Swag Box: Keyboard, Stickers, Pins, Towel, etc (Top 30)
The three winner grants run for 12 months from the close of the hackathon. The free event-window access does not count against them, so the top-three winners get a free year of Pro-API on top of the three weeks everyone gets.

All participants will be granted Startup-Tier CMC API, after they start the register to participate in the CMC API Hackathon. The free Startup-Tier API will persist until the end of the Hackathon period; coinciding with their final project BUIDL submission.

Notable projects will have their Tweets retweeted by the official CoinMarketCap X/Twitter account with +7.1M followers.

Judging Criteria
Each track is scored out of 100. Weightings are published per track on the Tracks tab.

Does it work: 30
Usefulness to a real person: 25
Interesting use of the API: 20
Code quality and documentation: 15
Presentation: 10
Rules
Eligibility

Open worldwide, subject to the shipping exclusions below
You must be 18 or over
Solo or team entries, teams up to 4
Originality

Work must be original and built for this hackathon
Pre-existing projects are eligible only if the CMC integration is new and clearly identified
Open-source dependencies and AI coding assistants are fine, and you do not need to declare them
Submitting someone else's work will get you disqualified
API Keys and Access

Every participant receives a free Startup tier plan for the duration of the event
Event access ends when submissions close and reverts to the free Basic tier
The three winner grants are separate: 12 months of free access starting the day the hackathon closes, so the event weeks do not count against your year
Top 3 and Top 30 is decided by judged rank. If more than 30 valid submissions are received, boxes go to the 30 highest scoring
Use your own key, issued through the campaign signup
Do not share, publish or resell keys
Do not commit keys to your public repo. We will ask you to rotate it and it will count against code quality
How to get Free Startup-tier API (during the Hackathon)

Create a free CoinMarketCap API account at coinmarketcap.com/api if you do not have one.
Register for the hackathon on DoraHacks. The registration form asks for the email address on your CoinMarketCap account.
Submit that CoinMarketCap account email. It must match the email on your CoinMarketCap account, not necessarily the one you use for DoraHacks.
Your account is upgraded to the free Startup tier for the hackathon period, on your own existing API key.
Shipping and prizes

Swag ships worldwide except to excluded jurisdictions: Afghanistan, Belarus, Bolivia, Crimea (Ukraine), Cuba, Donetsk (Ukraine), Iran, Iraq, Libya, Luhansk (Ukraine), Myanmar, Nepal, North Korea, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, and Yemen.
Winners are screened against applicable sanctions lists before results are announced
If we cannot legally ship to you, the API access portion of your prize still stands
Winners must supply a shipping address within 14 days of the announcement
Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash
We collect names and shipping addresses solely to send prizes, and delete them after shipping.
Judging and rights

Judging decisions are final
CMC may feature, repost and showcase submitted work with credit
You keep all ownership of what you build
Multiple submissions are allowed and encouraged, but one prize per person or team
Conduct

Be decent to other participants
Vote manipulation, spam or misrepresenting your submission means disqualification
API Feedback
Tell us where the API got in the way. Missing fields, confusing docs, rate limits that made no sense. That feedback goes straight to the product team and it is worth more to us than any single submission. We will publish what we heard and what we are doing about it.
